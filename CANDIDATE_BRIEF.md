# Candidate brief

## Would you keep this product?

Work a shift at the campus media desk. Get the Film Society’s kit out for its screening, handle the return, and leave the next shift enough information to finish the job. Use the time control to move the story forward.

Show one moment the product handles well and one moment you would change. Use a specific action or decision from your shift as evidence for each.

Sketch or prototype your change. Walk us through the same job using it, explain what you would keep, and name one observation at a real counter that would tell you whether it helped.

Use any tools you like, including AI assistants, web search, and design or coding tools.
